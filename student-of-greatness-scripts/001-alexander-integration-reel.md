## Production Notes

### Filming
- **Location:** Same spot for all talking-head segments (consistency)
- **Lighting:** Key light 45°, slight shadow for depth — unromantic aesthetic
- **Camera:** iPhone 15 Pro / Sony ZV-E1, 4K 30fps
- **Audio:** Lavalier mic (Rode Wireless GO) — crisp, no room noise

### Editing
- **Pacing:** Cut on action/breath. No dead air.
- **Captions:** Burned-in, animated word-by-word for hook, then sentence-style. Yellow highlight on key terms (INTEGRATE, MIDDLEWARE, ARCHITECTURE).
- **Music:** None in first 42s. Low drone at LESSON. Outro sting only.
- **Color grade:** Desaturated, high contrast. "Unromantic" visual language.

### Platform Adaptations

| Platform | Hook Adjustment | Caption Strategy | CTA |
|----------|----------------|------------------|-----|
| **Instagram Reels** | First frame = text hook (no video) | 3-5 line caption with 10-15 hashtags (#alexander #strategy #builders #leadership #integration) | "Follow @studentofgreatness" |
| **TikTok** | Start mid-sentence: "...to INTEGRATE." | Native TikTok caption style, 3 hashtags max | "Part 2: Caesar's debt strategy 👇" |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Title card: "Alexander's Real Strategy" | SEO description: "Alexander integration strategy explained in 60s #shorts #history #business" | "Watch the 15-min deep dive 🔗" |
