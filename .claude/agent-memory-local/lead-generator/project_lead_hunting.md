Abdurrahman is building a cold-call lead list for PrintLoop SaaS by hand via Google Maps.

**The qualifier:** A business that appears on Google Maps but has NO website link in its panel. The missing globe/Website button in the action row is the key visual cue. These businesses exist and want customers but can't take orders/payments online — exactly PrintLoop's pitch.

**Scope (broadened 2026-06-13):** ANY local organization is fair game — restaurants, salons, tailors, pharmacies, hardware stores, repair shops, schools, event planners, catering — NOT just print shops.

**Geography:** Nigerian cities, university-adjacent and busy areas. Priority order: Lagos (Akoka/Unilag, Yaba, Surulere, Ikeja), Ibadan (UI, Bodija), Abuja (Wuse 2, Garki, Gwarinpa), Port Harcourt (Uniport, GRA), Enugu (Nsukka, Independence Layout), Kano (BUK).

**Why:** PrintLoop is an online order/payment platform for local businesses; v1 target was Nigerian universities. Currency NGN.

**How to apply:** When asked for lead-gen help, deliver plain-English, no-tech, no-API playbooks (Abdurrahman is non-technical). Goal volume is typically 100 leads. ~40-60% of NG small businesses have no website, so 100 leads ≈ 180-220 businesses clicked ≈ 2.5-3 hours manual work. Natural next deliverable: a 20-second cold-call opener script.
