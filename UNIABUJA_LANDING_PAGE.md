## DESIGN NOTES (For Carrd/Notion/Framer)

### Colors (PrintLoop Brand)
| Token | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Ink | #1A1410 | Headlines, body text, borders |
| Paper | #F8F4ED | Background |
| Persimmon | #D14B2C | Primary CTA, accents, links |
| Ochre | #C7944A | Secondary highlights |
| Sage | #6B7A5C | Success states, "live" badges |
| Fog | #888888 | Muted text, placeholders |

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Fraunces (serif) — bold, large
- **Body:** Inter — regular, 16px base, 1.6 line-height
- **Code/QR/Monospace:** JetBrains Mono

### Component Style (Editorial-Brutalist)
- **Buttons:** 2px Ink border, Persimmon fill (primary) / Paper fill + Ink border (secondary)
- **Hover/Active:** Hard offset shadow `5px 5px 0` — no blur
- **Cards:** Ink border 1px, Paper background, no radius (sharp corners)
- **Inputs:** Ink border 2px, Paper background, focus = Persimmon border
